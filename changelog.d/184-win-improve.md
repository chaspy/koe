### Windows
- Optional, off by default: the improvement loop (使うほど賢くする), the same mechanism as macOS. Turn it on from the tray (「改善ループ: オフ（有効にすると使うほど賢くなります）」) after a confirmation that shows what is sent and where. If `improveEnabled` is written as `true` in config.json, Koe shows the same confirmation at the next start and sends nothing until you choose Yes (No turns it off again); a changed `improveCommand` is confirmed again. Every `improveBatch` (30) new utterances, while Koe is idle, the recent transcripts (up to 60; no lock recordings, nothing that failed), the glossary terms and, like macOS, the matching part of the Claude Code session logs (`%USERPROFILE%\.claude\projects`: what was sent, the reply, the next message) go to `improveCommand` (default: your own Claude Code, `claude -p --model sonnet`). Misrecognized terms are added to `glossary.txt` (seen twice, or named explicitly), and each history record gets a judgement of whether it got through. Audio is never sent; 「改善ループで送る内容を見る」 shows the next request in Notepad without sending anything. After 30 pastes with the loop off, the tray status mentions the loop once (14 days apart, at most twice; never once `improveEnabled` is in config.json); 「改善ループの提案を出さない」 in the tray stops it. `koe.log` has counts only.
